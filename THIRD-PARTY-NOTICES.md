## Fonts: Barlow, Saira Condensed, Geist Mono

The UI bundles latin-subset woff2 files of three typefaces, at the weights it uses: Barlow (400, 500, 600, 700, and 500 italic), Saira Condensed (500, 600, 700) and Geist Mono (400, 500, 600). Each one is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL). The files come from the `@fontsource/barlow`, `@fontsource/saira-condensed` and `@fontsource/geist-mono` npm packages (5.3.0). The full licence texts, with each font's copyright line, are in `ui/src/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.

| Typeface | Licence |
